Strategy and deployment

A cross-product view of Microsoft security guidance changes related to Strategy and deployment.

Latest Strategy and deployment changes

15 November 2026

Adopt secure development planning imperatives

Strategy and deployment

The article now emphasizes planning and release expectations, expands its scope beyond application code, reorganizes risk areas, and updates its references and publication date.

15 November 2026

Development security implementation patterns

Strategy and deployment

A Microsoft Learn page now documents repeatable patterns for securing development environments, engineering workflows, repositories, toolchains, and delivery pipelines, with links to related guidance.

15 November 2026

Implement Overview

Strategy and deployment

The page now describes four connected components: business scenarios, security disciplines, technologies, and technical solutions. It also updates terminology, navigation links, and the table mapping business outcomes to implementation guidance.

15 November 2026

Microsoft Reference Architecture

Strategy and deployment

The page date changed from November 6 to November 10, 2026. A table heading and the wording about prioritizing security work were also revised.

15 November 2026

Microsoft Zero Trust Workshop - DevSecOps

Strategy and deployment

A Microsoft Learn page now describes the DevSecOps workshop pillar, its alignment with the SAF Development Security discipline, expected roadmap outputs, and implementation areas such as repository governance, developer access, secrets, and code review.

15 November 2026

Microsoft Zero Trust Workshop - SecOps

Strategy and deployment

The page updates its metadata and description, revises the SecOps introduction, and explains the distinction between the Zero Trust Workshop’s SecOps pillar and the SAF security discipline. Two reference links were also added.

15 November 2026

Plan and run SecOps incident operations

Strategy and deployment

The article was renamed and reorganized around incident governance and the lifecycle of planning, investigation, containment, recovery, and improvement. It adds guidance on accountability, decision authority, escalation, and human oversight.

15 November 2026

Plan cryptographic agility and post-quantum readiness

Strategy and deployment

Microsoft added SAF guidance on identifying vulnerable cryptographic dependencies, planning quantum-resistant replacements, and maintaining readiness as standards and threats evolve. It covers impacts across identity, applications, infrastructure, communications, IoT/OT, and supply-chain integrations.

15 November 2026

SecOps implementation patterns

Strategy and deployment

The Microsoft Security Adoption Framework now includes four SecOps patterns covering centralized visibility, standardized workflows, phased automation, and threat intelligence and hunting.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Business Scenarios Overview

Strategy and deployment

The page date changed to November 4, 2026, and the text now states that Microsoft Unified provides expert-led Security Adoption Framework workshops.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Discipline Iot

Strategy and deployment

The document date was updated, and several OT/IoT security role labels were revised, including Information Worker/Frontline Worker, Security Compliance Management and Organizational Compliance and Audit, and CISO, BISO, and Security Director/Manager.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Discipline Overview

Strategy and deployment

The page date was updated, and the Security Adoption Framework workshop description and link were revised to reference expert-led workshops through Microsoft Unified.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Discipline Posture

Strategy and deployment

The page adds guidance for applying posture management to AI security transformation, links posture decisions to critical assets and privileged access scenarios, updates role and technology-area descriptions, and refreshes related SAF links and metadata.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Discipline Strategy

Strategy and deployment

The guidance adds NIST CSF, SAF, CIS Controls, and Microsoft benchmark links; clarifies role ownership; adds KPI/OKR measurement, broader risk dimensions, change and problem management, and links to critical assets and lifecycle decisions. It also expands references to posture management, SecOps, incident operations, and related security disciplines.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Discipline Strategy Engage

Strategy and deployment

The page adds links to SAF overview and role guidance, corrects a typo, updates its date, and expands guidance on ownership, measurable feedback loops, and maintaining healthy security friction.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Existing Guidance

Strategy and deployment

The page adds the Zero Trust Workshop as an SAF-aligned experience, updates the Privileged access guidance link, and revises the accompanying resource description.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Model

Strategy and deployment

The guidance now links to AI security transformation resources and includes the Zero Trust Workshop alongside assessment and technology implementation guidance.

15 November 2026

Workshop Business Overview

Strategy and deployment

The page date and associated DevRel references were updated. The workshop table now links to a different workshop overview and implementation overview path.

15 November 2026

Workshop Business Security Leaders

Strategy and deployment

The guidance now connects the CISO Workshop, SAF, and MCRA; adds a post-workshop security improvement lifecycle, expected outputs, and audience-specific paths; and updates Microsoft Unified links and metadata.

15 November 2026

Workshop Business Security Leaders Video

Strategy and deployment

The documentation adds a framework relating CISO Workshop content to SAF and MCRA, maps workshop phases to follow-up guidance, and provides steps for turning workshop insights into a measurable security improvement roadmap.

15 November 2026

Workshop Zero Trust Overview

Strategy and deployment

The overview now describes workshop pillars as aligned with SAF technologies or disciplines, and refines terminology for workshop phases, sessions, and pillar owners. Author metadata and SecOps/DevSecOps participant guidance were also updated.

6 November 2026

Microsoft Zero Trust Workshop - SecOps

Strategy and deployment

The page title and heading now use “SecOps” capitalization, and the page date changed from May 24 to November 6, 2026.
