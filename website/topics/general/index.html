General

A cross-product view of Microsoft security guidance changes related to General.

Latest General changes

15 November 2026

Accelerate response and remediation in the Microsoft Secure Future Initiative (SFI)

General

The article was substantially reorganized around security outcomes, priorities, implementation lessons, and Zero Trust/NIST CSF alignment. It now highlights faster mitigation, response coordination, incident communication, and feedback into engineering and governance.

15 November 2026

Apply Microsoft Secure Future Initiative (SFI) lessons

General

The page was retitled and reorganized to present SFI as a source of lessons and priorities. It now directs readers to use SAF and Zero Trust to organize modernization, with product and architecture documentation for implementation. Identity and secrets guidance was recast around outcomes and priorities such as phishing-resistant authentication, managed identities, secrets protection, rotation, and exposed-secret detection.

15 November 2026

Avoid security antipatterns

General

The page title and heading changed from “Avoid common security adoption antipatterns” to “Avoid security antipatterns.” The document date was updated to November 5, 2026.

15 November 2026

Compliance Monitoring

General

The page adds guidance that security should be driven by business and security risk, and that effective practices support achieving and sustaining compliance.

15 November 2026

Configure and Tune the Zero Trust Assessment

General

Adds instructions to inspect, change, and persist assessment settings, including concurrency, cache, timeout, export, and dependency limits, with prerequisites and PowerShell examples.

15 November 2026

Connect to Zero Trust Assessment Services

General

A new Microsoft Learn page documents interactive and certificate-based connections to Microsoft 365 and Azure services, including prerequisites, permissions, tenant selection, and service-specific options.

15 November 2026

Discover top picks

General

The index adds a “Plan and improve your security program” section with links for Zero Trust, security posture assessment, architecture, incident response, and AI security. It removes the virtual training day promotion and updates several links and the page date.

15 November 2026

Enable secure remote work

General

The remote work article’s link to the security adoption model was corrected.

15 November 2026

Establish a Development Security discipline

General

The article was revised with updated descriptions, SAF context, planning links, and guidance connecting the DevSecOps Capability Model with security maturity assessment.

15 November 2026

Establish a Security Operations discipline

General

The article was retitled and its guidance reframed around establishing a Security Operations discipline. It now describes SecOps as detecting, investigating, containing, and supporting recovery from active threats, and clarifies that a SOC is one possible organizational structure.

15 November 2026

Foundational security laws

General

The article was retitled and reorganized to present 10 immutable security laws and 10 cybersecurity risk laws as guidance for decisions across the Microsoft Security Adoption Framework, including architecture and modernization.

15 November 2026

Get Started with the Zero Trust Assessment

General

The page now provides step-by-step setup guidance, including PowerShell 7.6+, Windows coverage requirements, permissions, the Visual C++ Redistributable, licensing, protected output, and telemetry decisions. It also notes that published packages may differ from the development source.

15 November 2026

Govern

General

The governance document now links to Security Adoption Framework guidance, including Security posture discipline and the SAF overview, replacing the previous Zero Trust adoption framework links.

15 November 2026

Guidance Smb Partner

General

The page now refers to the Security Adoption Framework (SAF) and Zero Trust Technologies deployment guidance instead of the previous Adoption framework and RaMP references. Related authoring links were also updated.

15 November 2026

Index

General

The page changes its section heading from Zero Trust to SAF and replaces six Zero Trust adoption links with SAF guidance covering posture, remote work, assets, incidents, and security discipline.

15 November 2026

Index

General

The Zero Trust landing page now offers starting points for security standards, reference architectures, AI security transformation, security products, and best practices. Its publication date was also updated.

15 November 2026

Integrate security solutions with Microsoft

General

The page now targets ISVs, software providers, and technology partners, replacing the Zero Trust overview with integration scenarios and links to product documentation for APIs, prerequisites, and publishing requirements.

15 November 2026

Map CIS Controls and CIS Benchmarks to SAF

General

Microsoft added documentation distinguishing CIS Controls v8.1 for prioritizing security practices from CIS Benchmarks for technology-specific configuration, and mapping both to SAF roles, disciplines, technologies, and implementation guidance.

15 November 2026

Map The Open Group Zero Trust model to SAF

General

The page maps The Open Group Zero Trust capabilities, security roles, and Architecture Building Blocks to Microsoft Security Adoption Framework disciplines, roles, and technology guidance.

15 November 2026

Microsoft 365 Zero Trust

General

The page’s framework references and links were updated from the Zero Trust adoption framework to the Security Adoption Framework (SAF), including renamed scenario links and a security posture discipline link. The page date and related reference links also changed.

15 November 2026

Microsoft security best practices

General

The page was retitled and substantially rewritten to cover Zero Trust, secure baselines, assessments, standards and frameworks, technology guidance, and secure engineering. It now emphasizes adapting practices to organizational context and using Zero Trust as a security foundation.

15 November 2026

Microsoft security products and platforms

General

The page now describes Microsoft security products and platforms across more technology areas, introduces the Security Adoption Framework, and replaces the security-outcomes list with product-family guidance and primary SAF relationships.

15 November 2026

Monitor and detect cyberthreats in the Microsoft Secure Future Initiative (SFI)

General

The article was rewritten to focus on detection quality, telemetry coverage, log retention, centralized access, anomaly response, cross-domain visibility, and Zero Trust/NIST alignment. Its former objectives table was replaced with priority areas and implementation lessons.

15 November 2026

Posture

General

The page updates its posture and framework references to the Security Adoption Framework (SAF) and adds separate guidance paths for security leaders, architects, developers, and practitioners.

15 November 2026

Prepare

General

The page now links to guidance on enabling secure remote work, protecting critical business assets, and minimizing damage from security incidents. Related documentation references were also updated.

15 November 2026

Protect engineering systems in the Microsoft Secure Future Initiative (SFI)

General

The article was revised to focus on trust across software development, build, release, and supply-chain systems, including priorities for asset governance, source-code access, pipeline standards, dependency controls, and engineering security signals. It also adds implementation lessons and updated Zero Trust and NIST alignment content.

15 November 2026

Remove the Zero Trust Assessment

General

Added a how-to page covering removal of assessment packages, dependency caches, output files, diagnostic packages, logs, and dedicated app access.

15 November 2026

Review Zero Trust Assessment Results

General

The page documents reviewing reports across seven Zero Trust pillars, investigating skipped checks and errors, and optionally importing mapped findings into Zero Trust Workshop task notes. It also describes report paths, prerequisites, data protections, and integration limitations.

15 November 2026

Run and Resume a Zero Trust Assessment

General

The page explains prerequisites, fresh assessments across all or selected pillars, output and progress options, directory behavior, and resuming processing from an existing export without collecting a new tenant snapshot.

15 November 2026

Secure Future Initiative updates

General

The page now directs readers to What's new in Microsoft security guidance for customer-relevant SFI progress and lessons. It also identifies SFI as Microsoft's internal security transformation program and points to the Security Adoption Framework for customer-facing guidance.

15 November 2026

Secure your foundations with SAF

General

A new Microsoft Learn page explains how to use the Security Adoption Framework and Microsoft guidance to address AI-era risks across identity, data, applications, infrastructure, posture, and governance. It also defines durable security outcomes and links to AI adoption and posture-improvement scenarios.

15 November 2026

Security Foundations for SAF

General

A new Microsoft Security Adoption Framework overview explains foundational principles and covers Zero Trust, post-quantum readiness, AI security, accountability, and Secure Future Initiative priorities.

15 November 2026

Security roles and responsibilities in SAF

General

The article was retitled and reframed around the Microsoft Security Adoption Framework (SAF), with expanded role definitions and responsibilities across leadership, business, risk, technology, operations, and security functions.

15 November 2026

Shift DevOps to DevSecOps

General

The article now emphasizes integrating security into normal development and operations work, and expands discussion of risks across source repositories, developer endpoints, build systems, identities, artifacts, dependencies, and cloud platforms. Its description, metadata, and supporting links were also updated.

15 November 2026

Strengthen security posture and compliance

General

The article’s link to the Microsoft security adoption model was corrected.

15 November 2026

Technology guidance for SecOps

General

The page title and wording now use “Technology guidance for SecOps” and “technologies,” reference SAF SecOps, and update links to technology and implementation-pattern guidance.

15 November 2026

Training

General

The training page now includes an “Apply what you learned” section linking to AI attack-technique and defense-capability catalogs, Zero Trust guidance, and development security implementation patterns. Its metadata date and AI-usage marker were also updated.

15 November 2026

Troubleshoot the Zero Trust Assessment

General

A new guide covers connection failures, skipped checks, slow runs, dependency issues, report problems, diagnostics, permissions, infrastructure scope, runtime settings, and certificate connections.

15 November 2026

Understand SecOps roles and operating models

General

The article now focuses on durable SecOps job functions, execution models, partnerships, accountability, decision authority, risk acceptance, and escalation paths. It also explains that SecOps functions may be performed across teams, providers, tools, and automation, with human accountability retained.

15 November 2026

Visibility Automation Orchestration

General

The page now refers to Microsoft Defender XDR, automated investigation and response, updated attack surface reduction guidance, Microsoft Defender for Identity, and Microsoft Sentinel using current Learn links.

15 November 2026

Visibility Automation Orchestration

General

The Microsoft Learn page describing partner integrations with Microsoft Sentinel for visibility, automation, and orchestration was deleted.

15 November 2026

What's new in Microsoft security guidance

General

A new page highlights customer-visible updates across Zero Trust, SAF, AI security, standards, best practices, and security operations, with links to related workshops, assessments, architectures, and framework mappings.

15 November 2026

Zero Trust as a security foundation

General

The overview now explains Zero Trust across identities, devices, applications, workloads, data, infrastructure, networks, and AI. It renames “assume breach” as “assume compromise,” and adds guidance on context-aware decisions, adaptive privileges, blast-radius reduction, detection, response, and recovery.

15 November 2026

Zero Trust Assessment Documentation

General

The landing page now covers Microsoft 365 and Azure configuration assessment across seven pillars, with added guidance links for permissions, service connections, running and resuming assessments, results, tuning, troubleshooting, removal, and AI coverage.

15 November 2026

Zero Trust Partner Kit

General

The Zero Trust partner kit documentation updates several referenced resource links and refreshes associated Visio download entries.

8 November 2026

How existing security guidance fits into SAF

General

The article explains how established Microsoft security guidance relates to the Security Adoption Framework (SAF), grouping resources as integrated, SAF-aligned, or companion guidance and mapping them to SAF disciplines.

6 November 2026

Microsoft Cybersecurity Reference Architectures (MCRA)

General

The page title and heading now omit “Review,” and the publication date changed from November 2 to November 6, 2026.

6 November 2026

Using SAF effectively

General

The article was retitled “Using SAF effectively” and now directs readers to entry points based on their objectives or organizational role. It replaces the previous top-down, build-up, and scenario-driven patterns with tables linking to strategy, architecture, disciplines, scenarios, technologies, workshops, and related guidance.
