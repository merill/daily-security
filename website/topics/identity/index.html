15 November 2026

Eliminate Identity Lateral Movement

Identity and access

The page date, related ontology links, introductory wording, and call-to-action text were updated.

15 November 2026

Implement Privileged Access

Identity and access

The page updates its metadata and reference links and adds links to privileged access overview, strategy, and architecture guidance before deployment details.

15 November 2026

Microsoft Zero Trust Workshop - Identity

Identity and access

The page now frames the Identity workshop around assessing identity posture, identifying gaps, and creating a prioritized roadmap across workforce, external, privileged, and workload identities. It also adds SAF alignment and reorganizes the implementation guidance.

15 November 2026

Phishing Resistant Mfa

Identity and access

The article’s date and reference links were updated. Wording about employee-lifecycle integration was revised, and the 92% coverage statistic and previous implementation callout were removed.

15 November 2026

Privileged Access Access Model

Identity and access

The page’s metadata and related guidance links were updated, including references to current Microsoft Security Adoption Framework guidance for establishing an Access and Identities discipline and building a privileged-access architecture strategy.

15 November 2026

Protect identities and secrets in the Microsoft Secure Future Initiative (SFI)

Identity and access

The page was reframed around reducing identity compromise, token abuse, and exposed-secret risks. It now highlights phishing-resistant authentication, reduced static credentials, managed identities, automated key and secret lifecycle controls, standardized SDKs, and faster credential remediation.

15 November 2026

Secure app identity with permissions and access

Identity and access

The page description now covers authentication, authorization, least privilege, API and token protection, and application identities. Metadata and several linked references were also updated, while the previous detailed guidance list was removed.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Discipline Identity Access

Identity and access

The page now describes the SAF Access and Identities discipline, expands security leadership roles, clarifies authentication versus authorization, and introduces identity, device, workload, data sensitivity, and risk signals for authorization decisions.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Discipline Identity Access Privileged Model

Identity and access

The page date was updated, and links were reorganized to guide readers from privileged access concepts through strategy, implementation, governance, and technical solutions.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Discipline Identity Access Privileged Strategy

Identity and access

The page date was updated from May 24 to November 5, 2026, and the strategy now links to guidance covering privileged access context, governance, and architecture design.

15 November 2026

Security Adoption Scenario Privileged Access

Identity and access

The page metadata and reference links were updated, and new guidance directs readers to the scenario hub, concept overview, and strategy and architecture resources.

15 November 2026

Security Concept Privileged Access

Identity and access

The page metadata and DevRel references were updated. It now positions the page as conceptual orientation and directs readers to guidance for governance planning and privileged access strategy and architecture.

15 November 2026

Security Operations Playbook Password Spray

Identity and access

The page date was updated from November 6, 2024, to November 4, 2026, and an extra closing parenthesis was removed from the ADFS Health Connect link.

15 November 2026

Security Operations Playbook Token Theft

Identity and access

The document date was updated to November 4, 2026, and an incident-response link now points to the “Detect and investigate” section instead of “Technical response best practices.”

15 November 2026

Zero Trust Assessment Permissions and Roles

Identity and access

Adds documentation covering API permissions, administrator consent, directory and workload roles, Azure access, and service-specific requirements for interactive users and certificate-based applications.

15 November 2026

Zero Trust Source Code Access

Identity and access

The documentation now focuses on stronger authentication, verified developer presence, least privilege, and governance for critical repository changes. Its metadata, references, and introductory text were also updated.
