Workshop Business Security Leaders Video

15 November 2026Doc update+31 / −2 lines

In brief

The documentation adds a framework relating CISO Workshop content to SAF and MCRA, maps workshop phases to follow-up guidance, and provides steps for turning workshop insights into a measurable security improvement roadmap.

What security teams need to know

Administrators can use the new mapping and steps to connect workshop outcomes with priorities, owners, architecture plans, and progress measurement.

The workshop is designed to support security and technology leaders as they align security strategy with evolving business priorities, technology platforms, and the threat landscape.

Use this relationship as your journey through the content:

  • Use the CISO Workshop to establish business and security outcomes and priorities.
  • Use SAF to organize and manage the security improvement program.
  • Use MCRA to plan and implement supporting security capabilities and architectures.

Tip

Our Security Adoption Framework (SAF) workshops align with guidance in our structured security adoption model.

If you're unable to organize an expert-led workshop, it's available as a series of publically available prerecorded videos summarized in this article. A pdf slide presentation of accompanying materials is also available for download/review.

How to use these videos in the SAF journey

Use videos by workshop phase, then move directly into program execution:
