SFI architecture

SFI architecture design focuses on a set of security principles that are adopted and integrated into Microsoft culture and governance. These security principles are applied to a set of focus areas (engineering pillars) by means of processes, standards, and continuous improvements.

Six SFI pillars,engineering pillars

SFI execution is organized into six engineering pillars:

  1. Protect identities and secrets
  2. Protect tenants and isolate systems
  3. Protect networks
  4. Protect engineering systems
  5. Monitor and detect cyberthreats
  6. Accelerate response and remediation

These pillars represent where Microsoft prioritized change in its own environment. They are not a replacement taxonomy for Zero Trust, SAF, or NIST CSF.

Relationship to SAF, Zero Trust, and NIST CSF

The SFI initiative focuses on six prioritized engineering pillars. Multiple objectives align with each pillar. Each objective represents a significant effort to improve securitySecurity Adoption Framework incorporates relevant lessons and reduce risk for Microsoft and our customers in a specific pillar area.

The pillars and objectives clearly align topractices from Microsoft's Zero Trust principlesSFI experience while connecting organizations to durable architecture, operational, and the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Frameworktechnology guidance for implementation.

  • SFI: What Microsoft prioritized, changed, and learned through security transformation
  • SAF: How organizations can structure and sustain security modernization based on their priorities and risks
  • Zero Trust: Security principles that guide strategy, architecture, implementation, and operations
  • NIST CSF: Cybersecurity outcomes and categories used to understand, assess, prioritize, and communicate cybersecurity risk

SFI practices apply Zero Trust principles:

  • Verify explicitly
  • Use least privilege access
  • Assume Compromised (Assume breach)

For more detail on how lessons from SFI connect to customer-facing SAF guidance, see How Microsoft Secure Future Initiative (SFI) informs SAF.
