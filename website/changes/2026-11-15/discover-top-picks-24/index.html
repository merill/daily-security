Choose the outcome you need, then continue from strategy and assessment to architecture, implementation, and operations.

  • Start with Zero Trust Apply Zero Trust principles across your security strategy, organization, and technology estate.
  • Run a Zero Trust workshop Bring business, security, and technology leaders together to align on Zero Trust priorities, architecture, and next steps using guided workshop materials.
  • Organize security adoption with SAF Use the Microsoft Security Adoption Framework to align business outcomes, security disciplines, roles, and technology work.
  • Assess your security posture Evaluate your tenant configuration and identify prioritized opportunities to improve security.
  • Plan your security architecture Use Microsoft Cybersecurity Reference Architectures to plan integrated security capabilities and modernization.
  • Prepare for incident response Use incident response playbooks to prepare, investigate, contain, and recover from common attack scenarios.
  • Secure AI apps and agents Discover, protect, and govern AI apps, agents, and the data they use.
